## Part 1: Agentic IDE Research

**Cursor:**

**What is it and who built it:**
Cursor is an AI-powered IDE, built by Anysphere, that is built upon the open source VS Code but adds many AI features. Unlike VS Code, it can understand a whole project instead of a single file.

**Main Features:**
•	AI chat inside the IDE.
•	For completing larger tasks, agent mode is available.
•	Editing many files at the same time.
•	Understands the whole codebase/project.
•	Code generation and debugging.

**Codebase Integration:**
•	Context is made better by indexing the whole project.
•	Supports MCP (Model Context Protocol).
•	Compatible with many extensions, including those used in VS Code.
•	Git integration.

**Pricing:**
•	Free plan available but with limited usage.
•	Pro plan for higher limits and more powerful models.

**Key Difference:**
Cursor is one of the best AI-first IDE with extraordinary understanding of large projects/codebases.

My Opinion:

•	**Strengths:**
o	Powerful.
o	Easy to use and great UI.
o	Very fast

•	**Limitations:**
o	Limited on free plan.
o	AI suggestions still need human review.

**Best Use Case:**
Building medium to large projects such as portfolio projects.

## Part 2: Prompt Engineering

This section contains five real-life scenarios, along with the prompts I engineered to solve each one using an AI assistant.

---

### Scenario 1: The Budget Trip

**Situation:** Planning a 3-day trip for 3 people in Pakistan on a total budget of PKR 15,000, covering transportation, hotel, meals, and sightseeing.

**Prompt:**
> You are a travel planner. Create a 3-day travel plan for 3 people with a total budget of PKR 15,000. The total budget must include transportation, hotel, meals, and places to visit in Pakistan. Choose a destination that fits within the budget. Show the cost of each day and make sure the total does not go over PKR 15,000. Give the plan day by day in a simple table. At the end, show the total cost and a few budget-saving tips.

---

### Scenario 2: The Difficult Message

**Situation:** Writing a polite follow-up message to a landlord about a broken water heater, while preserving a good relationship ahead of lease renewal.

**Prompt:**
> You are a message writer. Write a WhatsApp message or email to my landlord. My water heater has not worked for two weeks. Winter is coming. I already asked my landlord two times, but it is still not fixed. I want to be polite because I want to renew my lease and keep a good relationship. Write a short, polite, and respectful message. Ask the landlord to fix the water heater as soon as possible.

---

### Scenario 3: The Impossible Schedule

**Situation:** Creating a realistic weekly timetable balancing university, a part-time job, gym, a side project, study, and rest — based on energy levels rather than just free time.

**Prompt:**
> You are a time management expert. Create a realistic weekly timetable for my university, part-time job, gym, and side project. I am a 6th-semester student with 5 courses. I work part-time for 15 hours every week. I also have a side project that is due in 3 weeks. I want to start going to the gym 4 days a week, but I cannot follow the schedules I make. Create the timetable based on my energy levels, not only my free time. Provide a simple weekly timetable with time for university, work, gym, side project, study, and rest.

---

### Scenario 4: The Broken Student Meal Plan

**Situation:** Designing a healthy, budget-friendly 7-day meal plan for a student living in a hostel with limited kitchen equipment and a monthly food budget of PKR 8,000.

**Prompt:**
> You are a meal planning expert. Create a 7-day meal plan for me. I have a food budget of PKR 8,000 per month. I cook for myself in a hostel with only a stove and a small fridge. I want to stop eating instant noodles and fast food because they are affecting my energy and focus. Make sure the meals fit my budget and the kitchen equipment I have. Provide a 7-day meal plan with breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Also include a grocery list and keep everything within my budget.

---

### Scenario 5: Scholarship Interview

**Situation:** Practicing for a scholarship interview in 5 days, with a focus on managing nervousness, avoiding overly long answers, and receiving structured feedback.

**Prompt:**
> You are a scholarship interviewer. I have a scholarship interview after 5 days. I get nervous in interviews. Sometimes I forget what to say and give long answers. The interviewer may ask about my weaknesses, future goals, and why I should get the scholarship. Ask me one question at a time. After I answer, tell me what is good and what I should improve. Then ask the next question. At the end, tell me my strengths, weaknesses, and give me some tips for my interview.
